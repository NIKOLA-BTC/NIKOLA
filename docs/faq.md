# FAQ

## What is NIKOLA?

NIKOLA is a next-generation hashrate infrastructure platform designed to simplify access to Bitcoin mining.

---

## Do I need mining hardware?

No. Users can access Bitcoin mining through NIKOLA without operating mining machines or managing infrastructure.

---

## Are there electricity costs?

No. NIKOLA does not charge users electricity costs.

---

## Are there service fees?

No. NIKOLA does not charge service fees.

---

## Is there any commission?

No. Users keep 100% of mining rewards generated during the contract period.
